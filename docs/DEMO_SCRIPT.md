# RepoGuardian — 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Script

## Timeline & Talking Points

### 0:00 – 0:40: The Problem & The Space Singularity Boot
1. **Show the Opening Screen**:
   * Open `http://localhost:5173`.
   * Explain: *"Open-source maintainers are drowning in issues. RepoGuardian is an agentic AI assistant that continuously monitors repositories, identifies duplicates and regressions via project-aware RAG, and escalates critical threats."*
   * Point out the **Binary Black Hole Singularity Loader**: Vectorizing repository history and arming autonomous monitoring loops.

### 0:40 – 1:20: The 3D Git Branch Topology & Transition
2. **Show the Clean 3D Git Branch Tree**:
   * Point out the 3D Git branches (`main`, `release/v2.1`, `feature/auth-sessions`, `hotfix/terminal-escape`).
   * Click the glowing red button: **`[ 🚨 ENGAGE AGENTIC TRIAGE: SHOW ISSUES ]`**.
   * Watch the camera warp shockwave transition into the categorized 3D issue clusters.

### 1:20 – 2:10: Compulsory Requirements Walkthrough
3. **Multi-Step Agent Reasoning & Evidence-Backed Explanations**:
   * Click on a **Red Security Orb** (e.g. `#1812` / `#3` RCE): Show the evidence citing security keywords and 0-day response alerts.
   * Click on a **Magenta Regression Orb** (e.g. `#6`): Show the Project-Aware RAG card citing high cosine similarity to closed issue `#5` and the maintainer resolution note: *"Fixed in v2.1..."*.
   * Click on a **Cyan Needs Info Orb**: Show the automatically drafted follow-up comment asking for reproduction steps.

### 2:10 – 2:40: Agentic Monitoring & Real Subtasks
4. **Live Monitoring Activity**:
   * Point to the **Top-Left Monitor HUD**: Show the continuous polling radar, live subtask queue (`duplicate_check`, `missing_info_check`), and scheduler events.
   * Click **"Check Now"**: Watch the subtask queue pick up and process tasks live.

### 2:40 – 3:00: Human-in-the-Loop Feedback & Health Trends
5. **Feedback & Wrap-up**:
   * Click **👍 Confirm** or **👎 Override** on an issue: Show the human feedback confirmation and override tag.
   * Switch to **Health Trends** view: Show the Recharts graphs for backlog growth, response SLA drift, and 30-day contributor activity.
   * Conclude: *"RepoGuardian gives maintainers superpowers by turning raw issue noise into verified, evidence-backed triage actions."*
